# Janani localization readiness

Supported UI locale foundation:

- English (`en`)
- Telugu (`te`)
- Hindi (`hi`)

## Migrated surfaces

- Private Care Context
- Home / primary mother and partner navigation
- Home tool titles and captions
- Partner invite-code copy
- Non-medical background feature descriptions

## Deliberately not yet translated

Safety-critical and clinical wording remains in the source-approved language until the Telugu/Hindi versions receive clinical/content review. This includes emergency guidance, due-date escalation wording, diagnosis/medication disclaimers, condition-specific guidance, and other text where translation drift could change medical meaning.

## Next migration order

1. onboarding and authentication
2. pregnancy guide navigation/content shell
3. Health Profile and Health Tracker
4. Care Timeline
5. Food Guide
6. reminders and journal
7. Thinking of You and partner-specific screens
8. Settings, privacy and account controls
9. Care+ client UI
10. notification/widget copy

Billing remains deliberately deferred and is not part of the localization rollout.
