# Pre-existing diabetes and hypertension rule packs

Status: source-grounded draft pending qualified clinical review.

These packs are implementation safety specifications. They do not diagnose conditions, prescribe diet, set treatment targets, or recommend medication changes.

## Pre-existing diabetes
Janani may support logging, adherence to the user's clinician-entered plan, appointment preparation, and general education. It must not infer a diagnosis, invent glucose targets, prescribe carbohydrate/calorie amounts, or adjust insulin/medication. Personalized condition guidance stays disabled until clinical approval.

## Chronic or pregnancy-related hypertension
Janani may support accurate record keeping, trend presentation without diagnosis, and reminders to follow the maternity team's plan. It must not diagnose hypertension/preeclampsia, invent BP targets, prescribe sodium/fluid limits, recommend activity changes against clinician restrictions, or start/stop/change antihypertensive medication. Concerning symptoms/readings must route to the fixed urgent-care pathway rather than generative guidance.

## Review requirements
Before enabling either pack, record reviewer name/role, review date, version, approved statements, prohibited statements, and escalation policy. Any source or guideline update requires re-review.
