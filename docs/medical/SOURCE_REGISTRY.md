# Janani medical source registry

This registry records authoritative sources used to draft condition rule packs. It stores citations and implementation notes only; it does not reproduce source text. Clinical review is required before a rule pack can be enabled for personalized guidance.

## ADA-2026-DIABETES-PREGNANCY
- Organization: American Diabetes Association Professional Practice Committee
- Title: Management of Diabetes in Pregnancy: Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026
- Year: 2026
- Intended Janani use: source-grounding for diabetes-in-pregnancy safety boundaries, monitoring context, and clinician-led management principles.
- Licensing note: cite/link only; do not ingest or reproduce the publication as Janani training data without permission.

## ACOG-CHRONIC-HYPERTENSION-CHAP
- Organization: American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists
- Title: Clinical Guidance for the Integration of the Findings of the Chronic Hypertension and Pregnancy (CHAP) Study
- Status: reaffirmed March 2025
- Intended Janani use: source-grounding for pregnancy hypertension safety boundaries and clinician-led BP management principles.

## WHO-2025-MATERNAL-HEALTH
