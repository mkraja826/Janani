# Janani anemia and thyroid rule-pack drafts

Status: source-grounded pending qualified clinical review. These packs are not enabled for personalized guidance.

## Anemia

Primary sources used for this draft:
- ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 233: Anemia in Pregnancy (2021; reaffirmed 2024).
- WHO daily iron and folic acid supplementation during pregnancy guidance, updated 26 July 2024.

Implementation boundary:
- Janani may organize labs, supplement instructions, food education, and follow-up reminders.
- Janani must not diagnose the cause of anemia, prescribe supplement doses, or treat food advice as a replacement for clinical management.

## Thyroid disorders

Primary source used for this draft:
- 2017 American Thyroid Association Guidelines for the Diagnosis and Management of Thyroid Disease During Pregnancy and the Postpartum.

Implementation boundary:
- Janani may organize thyroid results, symptoms, clinician instructions, and follow-up dates.
- Janani must not diagnose thyroid disease, invent pregnancy-specific laboratory targets, alter thyroid medication, or interpret treatment adequacy.

## Review gate

Before any condition pack can set `enabledForPersonalization: true`, record:
1. clinical reviewer name and qualification,
2. review date,
3. source versions checked,
4. approved wording and escalation behavior,
5. next review date,
6. regression-test cases for unsafe advice and clinician-instruction conflicts.
