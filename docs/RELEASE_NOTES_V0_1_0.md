# Janani 0.1.0 — Internal Testing Release Notes

Janani is a pregnancy-support app for mothers and linked partners. This internal build focuses on gentle daily-care support, shared family reminders, private journaling, emotional connection, and offline reliability.

## Included

- Mother and partner onboarding with private invite linking
- Pregnancy week, trimester and estimated due-date progress
- Medicine, hydration, appointment, nutrition and custom reminders
- Local phone notifications and reminder completion history
- Reminder pause, resume, edit and deletion
- Private-by-default pregnancy journal with optional partner sharing
- Thinking-of-you messages, acknowledgements and partner push notifications
- Offline cache, pending-sync queue and manual retry
- Android home-screen care widget with deep links
- Data export, partner unlinking, leave-family and account deletion
- Safety, privacy and medical-disclaimer information

## Important limitations

- Janani is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prescribe or provide emergency care.
- Critical medicine schedules should always have an independent backup.
- A reminder created fully offline receives its local notification after reconnection.
- This build must not be promoted beyond internal/closed testing until Android compilation, two-device testing, privacy contact details and final visual assets are complete.

## Tester focus

- Complete mother and partner onboarding on two separate devices.
- Test reminder notifications and notification taps.
- Test journal privacy and sharing changes.
- Test partner messages in both directions.
- Test offline creation/editing and reconnect synchronization.
- Add and exercise the Android widget.
- Export data, disconnect/leave family, and delete disposable accounts.
- Report crashes, confusing language, privacy concerns, and notification delays.
