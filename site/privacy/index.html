Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Janani is a pregnancy-support application. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or emergency services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Janani may process account identifiers and email address; profile and family-role information; pregnancy dates and optional body measurements; reminder content and completion history; journal entries and sharing choices; partner messages and acknowledgements; and device notification tokens.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, calculate pregnancy progress, provide reminders, synchronize selected family information, deliver partner notifications, preserve journal entries, support offline synchronization, protect the service, and respond to deletion or support requests.

3. Family sharing

A partner gains access only after joining through a family invitation. Shared reminders and partner messages are visible inside the linked family. Journal entries are private by default and become visible to the linked partner only when the author explicitly enables sharing.

4. Storage and service providers

Janani currently uses Supabase for authentication, database, realtime and server functions, and Expo services for push-notification delivery. Information may be processed in regions used by these providers. We do not sell personal information.

5. Device permissions

Notification permission is used for medication, care and partner alerts. Android widget and reboot-related functionality may be used to restore reminders and show selected care information. Permissions can be changed in device settings.

6. Offline data

Janani may store a limited local cache and pending synchronization queue on the device. Supabase remains the authoritative shared record. Local data may remain until the app is cleared or uninstalled.

7. Data security

Janani uses authenticated access, Row Level Security, private family membership rules, protected server functions, and restricted push-token access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention, export and deletion

Users can export a JSON copy from Settings. Partners can leave a family. Mothers can disconnect a linked partner. Users can permanently delete their account in the app by typing DELETE. Deleting a mother account removes the associated family pregnancy space and shared records. Deleting a partner account preserves the mother's family pregnancy space but removes the partner account and data that depends on it.

9. Children

Janani is intended for adults managing a pregnancy journey and is not directed to children.

10. Medical and emergency information

Janani does not monitor symptoms or contact emergency services. Severe pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty, fainting, seizures or any emergency concern requires immediate contact with local emergency services or a qualified maternity-care professional.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as Janani changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are published.

12. Contact

Before public release, the developer must replace this paragraph with a monitored support email and, where required, a postal contact. Repository: Janani on GitHub.
