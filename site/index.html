Privacy, safety, and support

Janani supports mothers and invited partners with pregnancy journaling, care reminders, and gentle family connection. It is supportive and educational; it is not a medical device and does not replace professional care.

Medical emergency? Do not wait for an app notification or support response. Contact local emergency services or a qualified maternity-care professional.
