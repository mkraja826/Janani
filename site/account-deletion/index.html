Delete your Janani account

The safest and fastest deletion method is the authenticated control inside Janani. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete while signed in

  1. Open Janani and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings from the Home screen and scroll to Delete account. If the account is not in a family yet, choose Delete account on the onboarding screen instead.
  3. Enter the account's current password and type DELETE exactly in the confirmation field.
  4. Select Delete account permanently, read the final warning, and confirm.

The app submits the request only from an authenticated session. If it reports an ordinary error, the account has not been confirmed as deleted. If a request times out, its final result may be unknown because the deletion service can still finish after the device stops waiting; pause, then check whether the account can still sign in before submitting another request.

What deletion removes

  • Mother account: deleting the account removes its associated family pregnancy space and shared records that depend on that family.
  • Partner account: deleting the account removes the partner account and data that depends on it, while preserving the mother's family pregnancy space.
  • On-device data: sign-out and deletion cleanup is app controlled. You can also clear Janani's app storage or uninstall the app to remove app-controlled local data from that device.

Infrastructure providers may retain limited backups or security logs for periods governed by their operational or legal requirements. Read the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Delete without reinstalling Janani

This GitHub Pages site is information-only. It never asks for or processes your account credentials. If you no longer have access to the app, continue to Janani's separate static deletion page on its dedicated Cloudflare Pages origin.

Permanent action: account deletion cannot be undone. A mother's deletion also removes her family pregnancy space and dependent shared data. A partner's deletion preserves the mother's family space.

Open the secure account-deletion page

The secure page will ask for your Janani account email, current password, and the exact confirmation DELETE. Cloudflare serves the static page files, but the browser sends those credentials only to Supabase Auth and the protected account-deletion function; the form is not submitted to Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, GitHub Issues, or analytics. Check that the address is exactly https://janani-account-deletion.pages.dev/ before entering them, close the page when finished, and avoid using an untrusted or shared device.

A successful response may report that protected file cleanup was recorded and may continue asynchronously after the account itself is deleted.
