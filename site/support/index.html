Public support

Janani currently uses the project's GitHub Issues page for public support. Issues and their replies are visible to anyone.

Do not post sensitive information. Never include an email address, password, family invite code, pregnancy dates or measurements, health or medication details, journal text, message content, access token, device token, or screenshots containing personal data.

Ask for help

Describe the app screen, the action you attempted, what you expected, what happened instead, and your device model and operating-system version if relevant. Remove personal details from screenshots and logs before attaching them.

Open a public support issue

Public support does not promise an immediate response. For account removal, follow the separate account-deletion instructions.

Security concerns

Do not publish secrets, exploit details, or personal data in an issue. Until a private security-reporting channel is published, open only a minimal issue asking the maintainer to provide a private reporting method.

Medical concerns

GitHub support cannot provide medical advice or emergency assistance. Contact local emergency services or a qualified maternity-care professional for urgent or medical concerns.
