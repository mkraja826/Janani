Janani account controls

Delete your account

Before entering your password, confirm that the address bar shows https://janani-account-deletion.pages.dev/ exactly.

Use the email address and current password for your Janani account. Your browser sends them directly to Janani's Supabase project. Supabase Auth first verifies the email and password. The protected Janani delete-account function then receives the current password with the resulting access token so it can perform fresh verification before deletion. This page does not submit the form to Cloudflare, retain credentials, or store a session.

This action is permanent. Your account and associated Janani data will be deleted. Deleting a mother account also deletes its family space and dependent shared records. Deleting a partner account removes that partner's access while preserving the mother's family space. This action cannot be undone.

This web flow requires your current password. If you are already signed in to Janani, you can instead use the in-app account-deletion control.
